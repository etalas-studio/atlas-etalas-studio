Your Questions, Pertanyaanmu, Answered Terjawab

A quick guide to how Atlas turns context into action. Panduan singkat bagaimana Atlas mengubah konteks jadi aksi.

Does Atlas replace our existing tools? Apakah Atlas menggantikan tools yang sudah kami pakai?

No. Atlas connects to GitHub, Slack, Notion, Gmail, Drive, and other tools you already use — it replaces nothing. Tidak. Atlas terhubung ke GitHub, Slack, Notion, Gmail, Drive, dan tools lain yang sudah kamu pakai — bukan menggantikannya.

Is company data secure? Apakah data perusahaan aman?

Yes. Atlas tool access and actions are governed by escalation rules set from the start — clear boundaries on when human approval is required. Ya. Akses tools dan tindakan Atlas diatur lewat escalation rules yang jelas — batas kapan harus meminta persetujuan manusia ditentukan dari awal.

How long is implementation? Berapa lama implementasinya?

About 4 weeks across 3 phases: Foundation (~1 week), Knowledge & Integration (~1 week), then Execution (~2 weeks). Sekitar 4 minggu lewat 3 fase: Foundation (~1 minggu), Knowledge & Integration (~1 minggu), lalu Execution (~2 minggu).

Can small teams use Atlas? Apakah Atlas bisa dipakai tim kecil?

Yes. Start with one workflow — e.g., daily operations or knowledge ingestion — then scale as needed. Ya. Mulai dari satu workflow — misalnya operasional harian atau knowledge ingestion — lalu discale sesuai kebutuhan tim.

How is Atlas different from a regular chatbot? Apa bedanya dengan chatbot AI biasa?

Chatbots respond to questions. Atlas remembers context across sessions and directly executes actions through connected tools — it doesn't just give advice. Chatbot merespons pertanyaan. Atlas mengingat konteks lintas sesi dan langsung bertindak lewat tools yang terhubung — bukan cuma memberi saran.

How do I get started? Bagaimana cara mulai?

Choose Professional Course to learn and build yourself, or Consulting & Done For You to have us set up Atlas for you immediately. Pilih Professional Course untuk belajar membangun sendiri, atau Konsultansi & Do It For You kalau ingin Atlas langsung disiapkan tim kami.
